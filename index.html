How each tab works

Home — the whole month at a glance: income, spending, where the money goes.

Today — your money right now: what you saved, what you owe, what's safe to spend, and what's due next.

Calendar — tap a day to add a paycheck, bill, transfer or note. Set it to repeat and it fills in future months by itself.

Goals — the bigger picture. Tap a goal's status to switch between not started, in progress and done.

Savings — funds you're filling up (an emergency cushion, a trip), each with its own progress bar.

Debt — balances you're paying down, with interest and minimum payments.

Envelopes — a spending limit per category each month. Your spending fills them automatically.
